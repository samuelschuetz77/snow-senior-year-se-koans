## Scope limits

No specific instructor-assigned problem list was supplied. Coverage is checked against chapter sections, summaries, worked-method discussions, and representative problem types, not against an unseen assignment. Thirty-five questions cannot provide exhaustive practice of every end-of-chapter variant. These sets emphasize conceptual setup and quick arithmetic; longer calculator work (large amortization schedules, irregular IRR solving, extended growing-annuity exercises), real-company filing research/data cases, and exhaustive derivations may require targeted practice. Supplement from the actual assignment when available. Historical exchange rankings, interviews, and regulatory trivia are not memorization targets.
