2 Benchmarking: f_0024 chapter; opening explanatory passages f_0026 and f_0027. Controlled inputs, repeated timings, mutation, measurement boundaries, units. Python timeit documentation supplements callable support, number/repeat, and setup behavior: https://docs.python.org/3/library/timeit.html
3 Randomization: f_0035 chapter; f_0040 Fisher-Yates; opening load-balancing discussion f_0046. Uniform shrinking ranges, expected guarantees, seed reproducibility, patterned workloads. Security-generator distinctions are original clarifications.
4 Caching: f_0049 chapter; opening LRU structure discussion f_0053; f_0057 spatial locality. Map plus doubly linked recency, hits and eviction, locality. Key completeness, freshness, and Python reference layout are explicit implementation clarifications.
5 Red-black trees: f_0060 chapter; opening rules f_0063 and insertion f_0064. Ordering plus color balance, rotations, red insertion, repair. Conventional black sentinels and root normalization are stated explicitly; exact assignments and full deletion implementation were not inspected.
6 Treaps: f_0069 chapter; opening f_0070 and deletion f_0074. Key/priority distinction, min-priority orientation, rotations, deletion child choice. Expected height rather than an unconditional guarantee; priority ties and fixed-assignment uniqueness are clarifications.
7 B-trees: f_0077 chapter; opening I/O discussion f_0079 and B-tree structure f_0083. Block transfers, fanout, key ranges, splits, occupancy, equal leaf depth. Concrete layouts account for metadata and pointers; reads and writes distinguished.
8 External mergesort: f_0091 chapter; opening k-way merge f_0096 and M/B-way merge f_0097. Sorted runs, heap candidate selection, block refills, passes. Output buffers and both transfer directions are explicit refinements to the simplified model.
9 Monte Carlo: f_0100 chapter; f_0102 comparison with Las Vegas; opening Fermat testing f_0107. Sampling, error assumptions, one-sided witnesses, modular arithmetic. IMPORTANT correction: no universal 1/2 error bound for plain Fermat testing; Carmichael numbers pass all coprime bases. Primary additional reference: https://math.dartmouth.edu/~carlp/PDF/reliable.pdf . Conditional test error is not automatically posterior primality probability.
10 Randomized hashing: f_0110 chapter; opening division f_0113 and randomized hashing f_0114. Residues, patterned collisions, fixed per-table random mapping, collision handling. Prime modulus alone gives no universal uniformity guarantee; load factor and key-processing costs are clarifications.
11 Rabin-Karp: f_0117 chapter; opening sliding-window f_0120 and verification f_0126. Rolling summaries, positional hashes, collisions, exact verification. No unconditional 1/q collision claim; whole-search probabilities and key-length arithmetic costs require assumptions.
12 Bits: f_0129 chapter; opening vectors f_0132 and masks f_0134. Direct addressing, packed membership, bitwise set operations, universe size. Python arbitrary-precision storage and finite complement masking clarified with https://docs.python.org/3/library/stdtypes.html#bitwise-operations-on-integer-types .
13 Bloom filters: f_0140 chapter; opening f_0142 and multiple-hash f_0143. All-bits test, definite absence, possible presence, saturation, exact backing-store verification. Ordinary deletion unsafe; counting-filter and union compatibility are explicit extensions.
